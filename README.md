# Botanica

my botanical texture + spectral plugin. drop it on a pad, vocal, reese, whatever, and it blooms.

the short version: it pulls your sound apart by frequency and puts it back in your key. so noise and growl turn into chords instead of mush. there's six pages (Bloom, Grains, Spectral, Field, Ripple, Motion) and they each do their own thing, but Spectral is the heart of it.

this repo is just the **free demo**. it runs 3 days from the first time you open it, then the audio cuts out. no noise bursts or watermarks, it just stops. full version has no limit.

## download

everything's in [downloads](downloads) (or the Releases tab):

- **Windows** — `BotanicaDemo-Setup-1.0.0.exe` (VST3 + standalone)
- **Mac** — `BotanicaDemo-macOS-1.0.0.pkg` (AU + VST3 + app, works on M1/M2/M3 and Intel)
- **Linux** — `BotanicaDemo-1.0.0-linux-x64.tar.gz` (VST3 + standalone)

## install

**Windows** — run the .exe, next-next-done, rescan plugins in your DAW.

**Mac** — open the .pkg. if it moans about an unidentified developer, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and hit Open Anyway (one time, it's not signed yet). installs the AU for Logic/GarageBand and the VST3 for everything else.

**Linux**
```
tar xzf BotanicaDemo-1.0.0-linux-x64.tar.gz
mkdir -p ~/.vst3 && cp -r "BotanicaDemo-linux-x64/Botanica Demo.vst3" ~/.vst3/
```
then rescan. works in Reaper, Bitwig, Ardour, etc.

## a couple tips

- send MIDI into the plugin and play chords — Snap follows the notes you hold. this is the fun one.
- drag your own wav/mp3 onto the Field page to use it as the background layer.
